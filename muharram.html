What the nights held

  • Daily majlis

    Held every day from June 16 to June 28, 2026, beginning before Maghrib prayers, with Sheikh Hamza Saudagar.

  • Matam, dua and ziyarat

    Part of the program each day.

  • Food

    Served every night of the program.

“Hussain is from me, and I am from Hussain.”

Prophet Muhammad, peace be upon him and his family. Part of a longer narration reported in Sunan al-Tirmidhi and Sunan Ibn Majah.

Hospitality

Food was served nightly, and all were welcome. We warmly invited individuals and families to attend, learn and connect with the community.

Contributions of food, drinks, desserts and supplies are greatly appreciated. They help the center provide hospitality for both the brothers’ and sisters’ side.
