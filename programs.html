Programs through the year

Faith and community come to life through programs, education and shared celebrations. Dates change through the year, so ask the center for the current schedule.

Ask for the schedule

  • Muharram

    Lectures, supplications, remembrance and reflection on the life, sacrifice and message of Imam Hussain, peace be upon him. Food is served nightly and all are welcome.

    Muharram 2026

  • Ramadan

    Come together during the blessed month for worship, learning and community.

  • Eid gatherings

    Celebrate meaningful occasions and the joy of being together with friends and family.

  • Sunday School

    A supportive weekly learning environment for children to explore faith, Qur’an and Arabic.

    About Sunday School

  • Qur’an and Arabic

    Helping children build confidence with Arabic reading and proper Qur’an pronunciation.

  • Family gatherings

    Shared meals and family-friendly opportunities to build friendships in the Roanoke community.
