Our purpose

The Jaffery Center of Roanoke is a nonprofit Shia Muslim community organization. Our mission is to provide a welcoming space for faith, education, family and community. Everyone is welcome, whether you are a lifelong member, new to Roanoke, exploring Islam, or simply looking for community.

Through religious programs, Sunday School, educational events and gatherings, we seek to strengthen our connection to Allah, the Qur’an, and the teachings of Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him and his pure family).

We serve the Muslim community in Roanoke and the New River Valley. Our goal is to help families stay connected to faith, build friendships, and grow together in a welcoming Islamic environment.
