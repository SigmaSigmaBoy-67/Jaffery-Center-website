Room to learn at their own pace

Our volunteer teachers support children as they build a strong foundation in Qur’an recitation, Arabic reading and Islamic studies. Special attention is given to helping English-speaking students practice proper pronunciation step by step.

Classes are intentionally small, typically 6 to 18 students, so teachers can provide individual attention and encouragement.

We want children to build confidence, develop Islamic character, and form a meaningful connection with their faith, community and the Book of Allah.

What children learn

  • Read with confidence

    Students learn to recognize and read Arabic, with patient guidance that meets them where they are.

  • Connect with the Qur’an

    Lessons support recitation and proper pronunciation in an encouraging environment.

  • Live their values

    Islamic studies help children understand their faith and grow in character and community.

Questions from families

How do I register my child?
Use the sign-up page, or email jafferycenter@gmail.com to ask about registration, availability and the current schedule.
How big are the classes?
Typically 6 to 18 students, so volunteer teachers can give children individual attention.
Does my child need to know Arabic already?
Classes focus on helping English-speaking students build a foundation in Arabic reading and Qur’an pronunciation. Email the center to discuss your child’s needs.
How can we practice at home?
Regular short review sessions help children build confidence over time. Ask the teachers what your child is currently learning so you can practice together.
