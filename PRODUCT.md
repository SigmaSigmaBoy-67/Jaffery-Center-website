# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Redesign of an existing repo. Vite multi-page build on Vercel (`framework: vite`, `build:vercel`, output `dist`). The redesign keeps that deploy path working. Pages are static HTML and CSS with a small amount of JavaScript. Build-time image processing only.

## Users

Two audiences, one action each visit (confirmed by the owner): a Muslim family or newcomer in Roanoke or the New River Valley who wants to know where the center is and what is on, and a supporter or donor who wants to give. Both need the address, what happens at the center, and how to contact or give, within seconds.

## Product Purpose

Jaffery Center of Roanoke is a nonprofit Muslim community center. The site tells people it exists, shows what it runs (Muharram, Ramadan, Eid and other occasions, Sunday School for classes of typically 6 to 18 students (the sources give class size, not an age range), Qur'an and Arabic, family gatherings and meals), and lets them reach the center, register a child or give. Success means a first-time visitor knows where to go and who to write to.

## Positioning

A small, volunteer-run center serving Roanoke, the New River Valley and surrounding areas, rooted in love for Allah, the Qur'an, the Prophet Muhammad and his purified family. No neighbouring site can truthfully claim this address or this program list.

## Operating Context

Programs are seasonal (Muharram is a 13-night program held in the Islamic month; the 2026 run was June 16 to 28, 2026 with Sheikh Hamza Saudagar, daily Majlis, Matam, Dua and Ziyarat, starting before Maghrib prayers, with food nightly). Sunday School is run by volunteer teachers and registered by email. Community WhatsApp access is by email introduction. Donations are taken by Venmo, Zelle and PayPal. There is no online payment system or form backend on this site.

## Brand Commitments

Owner's instruction: keep every fact; rewriting headlines and generic copy is allowed. Existing assets to reuse: the Jaffery Center monogram and logo files in `assets/`, and the center's mihrab marks. The owner said to design original art if no real photographs of the center exist.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real facts above, from the repo pages and the original site (`research/FINDINGS.md`).
- No photographs of the center exist online. The original site uses AI-style stock art, a Muharram flyer and payment QR cards. Do not fabricate photographs, testimonials, attendance numbers, event dates, or a Zelle address.

## Product Principles

1. Address and next action first. A visitor never hunts for where, when, or how.
2. Every fact stays; nothing is invented to fill space.
3. Respect the subject. Arabic text and verses are set with care and not used as decoration.
4. Small center, honest scale: plain words, no marketing inflation.
5. Works on a phone in a hallway, with the screen read once.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Body text contrast at least 4.5:1, visible keyboard focus, readable Arabic (a proper Naskh face), reduced-motion respected. Visitors include parents, elders, and children's guardians reading on phones.
