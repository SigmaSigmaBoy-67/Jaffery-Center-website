Where it goes

Donations support Islamic education, youth programs, community gatherings, facility maintenance and the continued growth of a welcoming Muslim community in Roanoke and the New River Valley.

The Jaffery Center of Roanoke is a nonprofit 501(c)(3) organization (EIN 81-1064023). For tax or receipt questions, please contact the center directly.
